Common problems

My payment is still waiting
  1. Open Activity in your wallet and check the status.
  2. "Waiting" means we have not seen your Bitcoin yet. Check that your Lightning wallet sent the payment.
  3. If the invoice expired, create a new quote. Do not pay an expired invoice.
I paid but M-Pesa has not arrived
  1. Open Activity. "Processing" means your Bitcoin arrived and the payout is on its way.
  2. Check that the number is a Vodacom M-Pesa number and has no typing mistake.
  3. If it stays processing for longer than usual, contact us with your support reference, the number and the amount.
My invoice expired
  1. Invoices last 15 minutes and quotes expire sooner.
  2. Go to Send, enter the details again and create a new invoice.
  3. If you paid just as it expired, contact us with your support reference.
I entered the wrong recipient details
  1. Contact us immediately with your support reference.
  2. We will check whether the payout can still be stopped. Payouts that were already delivered may not be reversible.
I lost my wallet ID
  1. A wallet ID cannot be recovered. We only store a hash of it.
  2. Create a new wallet and save its ID using the download button.
  3. Payments in progress still reach their recipients.
