Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here is how we handle your information.

Information we collect

Your chosen display name, wallet ID, the M-Pesa number, initial and surname of recipients, and transaction amounts needed to process payments.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, ID documents or location data. We never ask for your Bitcoin private keys.

How we use your data

Data is used only to process payments, confirm delivery and provide support. We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the payment providers needed to complete a transfer.

Security

Wallet IDs are stored as keyed hashes and cannot be recovered if lost. Sessions expire after 7 days.

Cookies

BTC Pay sets one essential session cookie so you stay logged in. It is required for the service to work. We use no advertising or analytics cookies. Your choice on the cookie notice is saved in your browser storage.

Your choices

You can log out at any time and clear your browser data. To ask about your data, write to classicroykizzy@gmail.com.
