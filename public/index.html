Questions, answered

What is BTC Pay?

BTC Pay lets you pay with Bitcoin over the Lightning Network while the person you pay receives Tanzanian Shillings in their Vodacom M-Pesa account.

Do I need an email or a password?

No. When you create a wallet you choose a display name and receive a wallet ID. That ID is your login.

Do you hold my Bitcoin?

No. BTC Pay is non-custodial. You pay each invoice from your own Lightning wallet and we never keep a Bitcoin balance for you.

Which numbers and amounts are supported?

Vodacom M-Pesa numbers from 0740 to 0769 and 0790 to 0799. Each transfer is between 2,500 and 100,000 TZS.

How much does it cost?

The fee is shown in your quote before you pay, together with the exact amount in sats. BTC Pay adds no extra fee on top of the payout provider fee.
