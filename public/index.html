FAQ

Questions, answered.

Short answers about BTC Pay.

What is BTC Pay?

BTC Pay lets you pay with Bitcoin over the Lightning Network so the recipient gets Tanzanian Shillings in their M-Pesa account. Airtime purchases with Bitcoin are the second service.

What does it cost?

A fee of 2.2% is added on top of the amount you send, so the recipient always gets the exact TZS you chose. BTC Pay adds no extra fee of its own. The exact sats amount is shown in your quote before you pay.

Which M-Pesa numbers can receive money?

Vodacom M-Pesa numbers only: 0740 to 0769 and 0790 to 0799. Numbers on other networks are rejected before you pay.

What are the limits?

Each transfer must be between 2,500 and 100,000 TZS.

How long does it take?

After your Lightning payment is received, delivery to M-Pesa normally takes under a minute. We show Delivered only when the M-Pesa receipt is confirmed.

How long is my invoice valid?

A Lightning invoice expires after 15 minutes. If it expires, create a new quote instead of paying the old one.

Do I need an email or password?

No. You choose a display name and receive a wallet ID in the format BTC- followed by 16 digits. Your wallet ID is your key, so keep it private.

I lost my wallet ID. Can I recover it?

No. We don't store it in a recoverable form and don't collect an email or phone number to reset it. Save your wallet ID when it is shown.

Does BTC Pay hold my Bitcoin?

No. There is no balance to top up. You pay each invoice directly from your own Lightning wallet.

Which wallet can I pay from?

Any wallet that can pay a Lightning invoice, by scanning the QR code or pasting the invoice.

When can I buy airtime with Bitcoin?

Airtime is being added. Supported networks and fees will be shown at checkout once it is live.

My payment is stuck. What do I do?

Every payment has a short support reference. Email classicroykizzy@gmail.com or call +255614303771 with that reference and we will trace it.
